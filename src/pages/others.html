Awareness of the malaria vaccine in India

{{yearFromBib:singhal2022awareness}}

In an online survey of 2,371 adults in India, about 72% had heard of the malaria vaccine. Awareness differed by age, gender, education, residence, and caregiver status, pointing to the need to reach less-informed groups during a vaccine rollout.

TL;DR: Malaria vaccine awareness was uneven, so a rollout needs targeted public education.

Sleep hygiene and daytime sleepiness

{{yearFromBib:garg2019maintaining}}

We surveyed 375 male household heads in Rishikesh about their sleep habits, daytime sleepiness, and sleep quality. Lower socioeconomic status was linked to poorer sleep hygiene and more daytime sleepiness, while higher status was linked to poorer reported sleep quality.

TL;DR: Sleep problems differed across socioeconomic groups: poorer sleep hygiene and more daytime sleepiness at the lower end, but poorer sleep quality at the higher end.

Chasing engrams in zebrafish (Danio rerio)

{{yearFromBib:garg2022chasing}}

We assessed whether zebrafish can help us study the physical traces of memory. We compared the defining features of an engram in zebrafish based on the criteria proposed by Tonegawa et al. (2015). We reviewed the literature on zebrafish memory to assess whether they meet those criteria for the same. We also discussed why their small, transparent, genetically tractable brains make them a useful model.

TL;DR: Zebrafish may be a useful model for studying how memories are stored in the brain.

Introducing stimulogenetics

{{yearFromBib:garg2022introducing}}

We proposed “stimulogenetics” as a shared name for optogenetics, chemogenetics, sonogenetics, and magnetogenetics, then compared their potential for clinical neuromodulation. Less invasive approaches may be promising, but clinical evidence is still too limited to rank them definitively.

TL;DR: Defined a common name for cellular-level neuromodulation techniques and evaluation of their clinical promise.

Organizational Work

The Mind Gala

The Mind Gala was an initiative funded by the 2nd IndiaBioscience Outreach grant to encourage more students to explore neuroscience. I helped organize several talks for the student audience, including a panel discussion on careers in neuroscience. I ran a novel mentorship program for the students across India, pairing them with their peers and mentors from neuroscience and science communication community to write articles in neuroscience for the general public and school students. A collection of the articles were compiled in a book titled Tales of Neuroscience, which was published in 2023. The novel mentorship program was presented at the 2023 Society for Neuroscience Annual Meeting (Theme J).
